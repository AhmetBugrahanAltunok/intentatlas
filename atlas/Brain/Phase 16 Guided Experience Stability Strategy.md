# Phase 16 Guided Experience Stability Strategy

## Outcome

The Phase 15 source-to-atlas journey remains readable in a line-oriented PowerShell terminal and
visually stable through repeated Change Report and Fit Graph interactions. The work preserves the
production scanner, graph, ChangeReport, acquisition/cache, and immutable snapshot contracts.

## Delivery order

1. **16A - stable viewer geometry:** reproduce the owner observation against the exact pinned
   `pypa/sampleproject` revision, isolate the browser geometry cause, and freeze wide/narrow,
   mouse/keyboard, report/report-free regressions.
2. **16B - structured guided terminal:** retain every trust meaning while rendering one prominent
   IntentAtlas banner, named sections, wrapped evidence details, and one option per line in EN/TR.
3. **16C - integrated closure:** update the durable chain and user documentation, run every local,
   package, browser, network, vault, and remote-CI gate, then close the two owner-visible defects.

## Boundaries

- The report panel is still an overlay over one bounded SVG graph; no second frontend or layout
  engine is introduced.
- The terminal remains color-independent, line-oriented, screen-reader compatible, hostile-text
  safe, and dependency-free. Canonical IDs, enums, commands, flags, JSON, scope, revision,
  freshness, confidence, reasons, omissions, strategy, advisory, and `Tests executed: 0` remain.
- Local analysis is no-write and offline. Public acquisition retains Phase 15's explicit consent,
  bounded inert Git, exact revision, and managed-cache rules.
- The owner first-use observation is product feedback, not an independent Phase 11C observation;
  no human-usability or elapsed-time claim is made.
- Phase 11C, tag, release, publication, deployment, settings, visibility, telemetry, and
  announcements stay outside Phase 16.
