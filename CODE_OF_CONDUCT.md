# Code of Conduct

IntentAtlas is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community. Be respectful,
assume good intent, give actionable technical feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and
changes rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and repeated disruptive behavior are not
accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently
restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's security or
private contact channel. Reports will be handled confidentially and as promptly as possible.
